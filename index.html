Autoregressive Model for Financial Data

Modelling irregularly spaced high-frequency trades across FX, crypto and US equity with Autoregressive Conditional Duration (ACD) models.

  • 7.3Mtrade durations
  • 3markets & resolutions
  • 610K+test events
  • Built an end-to-end R pipeline (~5,600 lines) to ingest and clean tick data from 3 sources at millisecond, microsecond and nanosecond resolution (FX, crypto, US equity), producing 7.3M trade durations over a 14-day training window.
  • Diagnosed a data-quality artefact affecting 49% of NVDA and 19% of BTCUSDT durations (sub-millisecond records), traced it to exchange batch-stamping (8,095 fills sharing one timestamp), and corrected it through event consolidation.
  • Fitted and compared 4 time-series model specifications (Exponential, Weibull, Log-ACD 1 and Log-ACD 2), assessing goodness-of-fit using AIC/BIC and residual diagnostics.
  • Removed intraday seasonality using spline-smoothed 5-minute adjustment fitted on training data only, eliminating leakage into the test set.
  • Showed that unstable persistence estimates on 2 of 3 assets were driven by how near-simultaneous trades were treated rather than by sample size, and defended this finding at viva.
  • R
  • ACD Models
  • Maximum Likelihood Estimation
  • Time-Series Forecasting
